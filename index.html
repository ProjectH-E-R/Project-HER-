A little about us

What Project H.E.R. is all about

Problem

Many women with young children experience financial hardship and struggle to access menstrual products, general hygiene supplies, and diapers consistently. Rising costs can make these necessities even harder to afford.

Goal

Our goal is to provide essential feminine and general hygiene products, as well as infant-care products, to women and children experiencing financial hardship by partnering with local organizations to ensure donations meet the needs of the people they serve.
